Laud Ochei Charles, PhD

Head of Education

DeepSlate

An expert in executive education with a distinguished track record of designing and delivering high-impact leadership programmes for senior audiences across industry and the public sector.

With a doctorate grounding the work in learning theory and adult education research, the approach balances academic rigour with real-world applicability — building programmes that equip leaders not just with knowledge, but with the capability to act on it.

At DeepSlate, this means developing education experiences that are intellectually serious, practically grounded, and designed for the specific challenges facing leaders navigating AI and digital transformation.

The best executive education does not just inform. It changes how people see their role — and gives them the confidence to lead differently.

Selected areas of work

  • Design and delivery of AI literacy programmes for board-level and C-suite audiences
  • Curriculum development for multi-module digital leadership academies
  • Facilitation of executive masterclasses across financial services, healthcare, and government
  • Partnership development with universities and professional bodies on accredited programmes
