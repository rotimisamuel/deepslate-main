Gold Samuels

AI Strategy Lead

DeepSlate

A specialist in AI governance and data strategy, advising senior executives and boards on how to adopt AI responsibly, govern it rigorously, and communicate about it credibly. The focus is on the intersection of technical capability and institutional accountability — an area where the stakes are high and the expertise genuinely scarce.

Most AI governance failures happen not because organisations lack good intentions, but because they lack the structures, the language, and the decision-making frameworks to act on them. The work here is to build those structures — practical, proportionate, and designed to actually be used rather than filed away.

"Good AI governance is not about slowing things down. It is about knowing what you are doing and why — so that when things go wrong, you can respond with clarity rather than panic."

With a background spanning technology, policy, and organisational strategy, this role brings an unusual breadth to AI governance work. The regulatory environment is evolving rapidly — the EU AI Act, emerging UK frameworks, sector-specific guidance — and clients value having an advisor who tracks these developments closely and translates them into practical action.

Selected areas of work

  • AI governance framework design for NHS Trusts, financial institutions, and government bodies
  • AI ethics policy development and responsible AI programme design
  • Data strategy and data maturity assessment for organisations preparing for AI adoption
  • Board-level advisory on AI risk, vendor assessment, and regulatory readiness
